# Can a middle-aged dog learn new tricks?

I’m Ben. I’m 43, a dad, and I work in community in the cybersecurity space. After years in IT and cybersecurity, I’ve decided to become a beginner at something again.

Apparently, I needed another reason to stare at a computer.

I’m learning Python and exploring data analytics and AI. This is where I’ll write about how that goes, including the things I build, the things I break, and whatever finally makes sense after reading it twelve times.

## Why am I doing this?

Part curiosity, part challenge, and part wanting to see what I can do with the experience I already have.

I’ve spent years troubleshooting systems, spotting patterns, and helping people find useful information. Working in community has made me even more interested in the questions behind the numbers. What are people struggling with? Are they finding answers? What are we missing?

I want to get better at digging into those questions myself. Taking messy information, figuring out whether I can trust it, and turning it into something useful has always appealed to me.

And with AI showing up in just about everything, understanding the data behind it feels like a pretty good place to spend some time.

**Slop in, slop out.** Giving a bad spreadsheet an AI assistant doesn’t magically make it a good spreadsheet.

## There will probably be some math complaints

I’ve never thought of myself as a math person. That’s probably part of why I haven’t tried this sooner.

But I don’t need advanced math to start writing Python or cleaning up data. As I get deeper into analytics and machine learning, I’ll have more math to learn. I’m willing to give it another shot, especially when I can see what I’m using it for.

Who knows. Maybe math and I just needed some time apart.

## What you’ll find here

Mostly, notes from someone figuring things out.

I’ll write about what I’m learning, share small projects, and keep track of the explanations that help things click. Some posts might be useful. Others might just be me celebrating a script that finally ran without an error.

I’m calling this a garden because I expect to come back and change things. A note I write today might need fixing next month. If I get something wrong, I want to understand why and update it.

I also have kids, a job, and the regular adult responsibilities that rudely continue while I’m trying to learn something. Some weeks I’ll make good progress. Other weeks, getting one little program working will be enough.

I’ll document both.
